# 英文钩子库（前 3 秒定生死）

> 模式 A 写 hook、模式 C 拆对标时读这个文件。
> 每条视频给 3 个不同类型的 hook 供选择。Hook = 第一句话 + 第一帧画面 + 首屏字幕，三件套要一起设计。

## 十大钩子类型

### 1. 禁忌/警告反转（Forbidden）— 认知反差类目首选
- "I was told not to come to China. I came anyway."
- "My government has a travel advisory for this place. Here's what it actually looks like."
- "Everyone warned me about China. Nobody warned me about THIS."（THIS = 正面惊喜）
- 首帧配合：本人回头看镜头走进夜市/街头人流

### 2. 价格冲击（Price Shock）— 花费类目首选
- "This entire meal cost me $3."
- "I paid $85 for this hotel room. Look at this."
- "Guess how much this 200 mph train ticket cost."
- 首帧配合：手里拿实物/账单怼镜头，字幕大号数字

### 3. 政策急迫（News/Urgency）— 政策类目首选
- "You can now visit China for 10 days without a visa — and almost nobody knows."
- "This changed in [月份] and it makes China trips 10x easier."
- "Before you book ANY flight to Asia, watch this."
- 首帧配合：护照+登机牌，或入境章特写

### 4. 已知 IP 借力（Familiar Anchor）— 奇观目的地首选
- "Avatar didn't invent these mountains."
- "This city looks like Cyberpunk 2077 in real life."
- "Blade Runner was filmed in a studio. This is just a Tuesday in Chongqing."
- 首帧配合：最炸裂的一帧景观直接开画

### 5. 挑战开局（Challenge）
- "$100 for 3 days in Shanghai. Let's see how far it goes."
- "I have 24 hours and $50 in Chengdu. Day starts now."
- "Can I survive a week in China without speaking a word of Chinese?"
- 首帧配合：现金/手机余额特写 + 计时器字幕

### 6. 失误自曝（Mistake Confession）— 信任感最强
- "I made every mistake so you don't have to. Mistake #1 almost got me stranded."
- "I lost $200 in China because nobody told me this."
- "The one app I didn't download — and it ruined my first day."
- 首帧配合：懊恼表情/机场滞留画面

### 7. 悬念挑衅（Curiosity Gap）
- "There's a train in China that goes THROUGH an apartment building."
- "This Chinese city built a bridge higher than the Eiffel Tower. Twice."
- "What $10 gets you at a Chinese street market will upset you."
- 首帧配合：只给一半画面，答案 3 秒后揭晓

### 8. 身份代入（Identity POV）— 安全类目首选
- "Solo female traveler. 1 AM. Chinese city. Watch what happens."
- "I'm a Black American in rural China. Here's my honest experience."
- "First time in China with my 70-year-old mom."
- 首帧配合：第一人称行走镜头

### 9. 清单速给（Listicle）— 攻略类目首选
- "5 things to do BEFORE you land in China. Number 3 is non-negotiable."
- "3 apps that make China feel easy. 1 that's useless."
- "7 mistakes every first-timer makes in China."
- 首帧配合：字幕列表滚动或手比数字

### 10. 反向劝退（Reverse Psychology）
- "Don't come to China. You'll never enjoy your home country's trains again."
- "China ruined traveling for me. Everywhere else feels slow now."
- "Honestly? Skip China... if you hate good food and cheap massages."
- 首帧配合：故作严肃表情转笑

## Hook 写作规则

1. **第一个词不能浪费**："So today..." / "Hey guys..." 直接判死刑。用 I / You / 数字 / 地名开头
2. **7 秒内必须兑现一次**：hook 承诺的东西（价格/画面/反转）要在 7 秒内给到第一口，剩下的分批给
3. **字幕 ≠ 旁白**：首屏大字幕可以和口播不同——口播说故事，字幕给结论（如口播 "I was told not to come"，字幕 "CHINA: 4 AM STREET TEST"）
4. **同选题换 hook 重发是合法操作**：一条素材配 3 个 hook 各发一次（隔 2 周），是账号冷启动期的标准测试手法
5. **英文降噪**：hook 里禁 "amazing/incredible/unbelievable"——形容词是懒惰税，用数字和名词制造冲击
